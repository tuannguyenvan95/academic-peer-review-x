# Autonomous AI Consensus in Decentralized Zero-Knowledge Systems

**DOI:** `10.1038/s41586-026-00001`  
**Authors:** Dr. Alice Vance, Dr. Bob Miller  
**Affiliation:** Open DeSci Lab & GenLayer Foundation  
**Publication Venue:** Nature Computer Science / Academic Open Archive  
**Status:** Peer-Reviewed & Accepted  

## Abstract
This paper introduces an optimistic subjective consensus protocol executed directly inside GenVM. By anchoring non-deterministic web verification and LLM peer-review jury evaluations to discrete classification tiers, we prove that subjective agreements reach deterministic economic finality without cross-validator divergence.
